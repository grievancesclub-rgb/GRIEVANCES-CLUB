Who we are

This is where the Grievance Club comes in.

Our motto: "Every Voice Deserves to be Heard." We are a platform where students come together to talk about their concerns and work towards real, effective solutions.

We are a student-driven group dedicated to fairness. We promise two things — every voice will be heard, and every concern will be addressed respectfully, professionally, and fairly. We are the clear link between you and the administration.

Promise 01

Every voice will be heard.

Promise 02

Every concern will be addressed respectfully, professionally, and fairly.

Our commitment

Our mission is simple: to make your academic life better and more fair.

"Think of us as the bridge that connects you to the right people for help and resolution."

An easy-to-use platform

A simple, accessible way to raise complaints — no confusing steps, no runaround.

Awareness of your rights

We help you understand what you're entitled to as a student, so you can advocate for yourself with confidence.

Less stress, more support

You don't have to navigate a difficult situation alone — we carry the weight of the process with you.

Our process

Clear, confidential, and followed through — every time.

01

Submit confidentially

You submit your concern confidentially, in your own words, whenever you're ready.

02

Review & document

The Club reviews and documents the issue carefully, so nothing gets lost or overlooked.

03

Facilitate discussion

We facilitate a fair discussion with the relevant department on your behalf.

04

Follow up

We follow up until the problem is genuinely solved — not just closed on paper.

What we help with

No concern is too small to raise.

Grievances come in many forms — academic, administrative, or personal. If it's affecting your experience on campus, it belongs on our desk.

Academic & Examination

Grading, evaluation, coursework, and exam-related concerns.

Administrative

Fees, documentation, timelines, and process-related issues.

Campus & Facilities

Infrastructure, hostel, classrooms, and day-to-day campus life.

Interpersonal

Conflicts or concerns involving peers, faculty, or staff.

Safety & Wellbeing

Anything affecting your sense of safety or wellbeing on campus.

Something else?

If it doesn't fit neatly into a category, tell us anyway — we'll listen.
